## Domain guardrails

- Preserve the finite handcrafted delivery core: rail encounter order, unlimited LIFO/TOP,
  manual load default with auto toggle, persistent branch/occupied lock, fresh-state Retry versus Edit.
- SX-DEC-060: pickup is exact-cell; station delivery is one cardinal-adjacent cell,
  not diagonal or station footprint. Preflight checks the start-reachable RUN component,
  not whether every irrelevant rail island connects. Read actual approved owner for exact semantics.
- Reuse current modules before adding layers; mutable values belong to structured project data.
- Do not revive endless fuel/BOOST/capacity/respawn, unapproved score/generators, or a solver.
- Preserve approved overhead assets, consumer geometry, engine and storage compatibility.
  No new raster without a verified consumer; follow current image generation/approval policy.
